Mzansi Travel Guide

Your gateway to exploring South Africa

Site Name

Mzansi Travel Guide

This name reflects a proudly South African travel platform that highlights beautiful destinations, culture, and tourism opportunities across the country.

Optional domain availability: mzansitravelguide.co.za

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide visitors with travel information about popular destinations in South Africa. The site will include destination highlights, travel tips, cultural insights, and contact information for tourism services.

Scenarios

  • What are the best tourist attractions to visit in South Africa?
  • Where can I find travel tips for visiting Cape Town or Durban?

Color Schema

Primary Color: #006d77 (Teal) – Used for headings and navigation background.

Secondary Color: #ffb703 (Golden Yellow) – Used for accents, buttons, and highlights.

Neutral Background: #f1faee – Used for page background.

Typography

Montserrat: Used for headings (h1, h2, h3).

Open Sans: Used for body text and paragraphs.
